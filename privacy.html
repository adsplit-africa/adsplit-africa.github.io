Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version: 2.5 • Application: AdSplit Rewards

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which AdSplit Rewards ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("User" or "you") of the AdSplit Android mobile application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in all our data handling practices. By accessing or using AdSplit Rewards, you signify your acceptance of this policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Identification Information

We may collect personal identification information from Users in a variety of ways, including when Users register, sign in with Google Identity Services, or request reward cash-outs:

  • Account Authentication: Email address, user ID (Firebase UID), and display name provided during Google Sign-In or email registration.
  • Disbursement / Cash-Out Details: Mobile wallet recipient phone numbers (e.g. M-Pesa) or payment recipient addresses provided strictly upon user-initiated withdrawal requests.

B. Non-Personal & Technical Information

When you interact with our Application, we may collect technical data to ensure service integrity, prevent automated fraud, and serve advertisements:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID), operating system version, and general hardware profile.
  • Network Information: General connection state (online/offline status) to synchronize ledger state.
  • Application Ledger Logs: Transaction timestamps, game level progress (Nut Sort 3D), completed ad sessions, and cryptographic verification tokens.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The Application utilizes trusted third-party services that process data under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for platform APIs, account credential management, and app integrity verification. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers and location signals to deliver personalized and non-personalized advertising. (AdMob & Google Ad Policies)
  • Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Used for secure user authentication, immutable double-entry ledger persistence, and server-side verification callbacks. (Firebase Privacy Policy)

4. Server-Side Verification (SSV) & Anti-Fraud

To prevent fraudulent crediting, AdSplit employs Google AdMob Server-Side Verification (SSV). When you watch a rewarded ad break, AdMob servers communicate directly with our Cloud Functions via an ECDSA cryptographically signed webhook. We verify:

  • That the ad impression was fully rendered to a human user.
  • That the daily ad quota (e.g. 10 ads per Kenya business day) is strictly respected.
  • That replayed or modified transaction identifiers are automatically rejected.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information, user ID, transaction tokens, and ledger data.

Strict Policy: We do NOT sell, trade, or rent Users' personal identification information to third parties. All financial ledger records are used strictly for user balance accounting and compliance auditing.

6. Account & Data Deletion Rights

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Legal & Account → scroll to Danger Zone → tap Delete Account Permanently.
  • Manual Web Request: You may also request account deletion by emailing our support team at mikemercy623@gmail.com with your registered email and Firebase UID.

Upon receiving a deletion request, your authentication credentials, personal details, and pending withdrawal records will be permanently purged within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

AdSplit Rewards is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such records.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

AdSplit Rewards reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. When we do, we will revise the updated date at the top of this page. We encourage Users to frequently check this page for any changes to stay informed about how we protect their personal data.
