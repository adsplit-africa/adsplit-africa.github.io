About AdSplit

Next-Generation Digital Rewards Platform • Nairobi, Kenya

1. Our Mission

AdSplit Rewards was founded with a clear, equitable purpose: to bridge the gap between digital advertising revenue and the users whose attention makes that revenue possible. We empower users across Africa and globally to turn everyday casual mobile engagement into verified USD income through fair, transparent reward sharing.

2. What We Offer

AdSplit brings entertainment, utility, and earning opportunities into one unified mobile experience:

  • Watch & Earn: Verified rewarded video ads powered by Google AdMob, crediting real USD rewards to user accounts upon validated completion.
  • Listen & Earn: Curated online radio stations and podcasts with periodic sponsored audio ad rewards.
  • Casual Games (Nut Sort 3D, Scratch & Spin): Engaging color-sorting puzzles and daily challenge games that reward puzzle mastery and progression.
  • Dual-Ledger Accounting: Real-time, transparent tracking of verified rewards balance, pending transactions, and lifetime earnings.

3. Fair Play & Security Sentinel

Google AdMob Server-Side Verification (SSV): All rewarded ad credits are audited via cryptographically signed ECDSA callbacks directly from Google AdMob servers to our backend infrastructure.

We believe in honest, genuine user engagement. Automated click farms, bot emulation, VPN abuse, and fraudulent request manipulation are automatically intercepted by our security sentinel to ensure platform sustainability and advertiser trust.

4. Withdrawals & Fee Transparency

Users who reach the minimum threshold of $10.00 USD can request payouts directly via:

  • M-Pesa: Direct mobile money disbursement.
  • Bank Transfer: Local and regional bank wire.
  • Airtm: Digital USD wallet disbursement.
  • MiniPay: Stablecoin / Celo disbursement.

Transparency Note: In line with standard merchant disbursement practices, third-party carrier tariffs and currency exchange spreads are deducted from the user's requested gross withdrawal amount (e.g., a $10.00 USD withdrawal will deliver the net remaining balance, typically ~$9.60 USD or local currency equivalent).
